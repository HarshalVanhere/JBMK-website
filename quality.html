Quality Gates

Process-Integrated Quality Assurance

Quality checks are integrated at every physical milestone, from material gate entry to outbound shipping container seals.

🔍

1. Incoming Inspection

Raw steel coils, bearings, and fasteners undergo spectroscopic material analysis, hardness checking, and thickness control prior to release into stampings.

📐

2. In-Process SPC

Operators perform dimensional checks using vernier gauges and digital indicators at periodic intervals, plotting data on Statistical Process Control (SPC) charts.

✅

3. Final Metrology & balancing

Critical dimensions are mapped on Coordinate Measuring Machines (CMM). Dynamic balancing systems verify zero rotational vibration at target RPM.
