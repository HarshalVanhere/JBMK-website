Crankshaft Pulley

Heavy-duty design to handle peak engine torque and minimize vibration. Also serving as the primary driver for all front-end accessory drives (FEAD), our crankshaft pulleys are manufactured to withstand torsional stresses and thermal variations.

Performance Engineering Highlights

  • Robust single-piece or multi-component spun construction
  • High torsional stiffness to handle engine crankshaft harmonics
  • Engineered for minimal mass and rotational inertia
  • Precision-machined keyways and mounting hubs

Standard Applications

  • Internal Combustion Engines (Petrol & Diesel)
  • Passenger Cars & Commercial Vehicles
