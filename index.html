Welcome to

JBMK Precision Components India Pvt. Ltd.

(Formally known as Jbm Kanemitsu Pulley Pvt. Ltd.)

Welcome to JBMK Precision Components India Pvt. Ltd. We are a technologically advanced precision engineering company with experienced capabilities in the manufacturing of industrial pulleys and power transmission components — whether it be in precision machining, formed metal, or multiple component assemblies. Our product range spans flat belt to multi-groove V-belt pulleys, timing pulleys, taper lock systems, and custom-engineered solutions meeting the highest industrial standards. Internal capabilities extend from product design, converting castings to lightweight precision components, tool & die design, machine design, to complete process engineering. We are a chosen global supplier, shipping to locations domestically and around the world including USA, Europe, and Brazil.
