Company Overview

Engineered for Performance.
Built for Reliability.

For over a decade, JBMK Precision Components (formerly known as JBM Kanemitsu Pulley Pvt. Ltd.) has been at the leading edge of industrial pulley design and manufacture. We deliver world-class power transmission solutions from lightweight stamped metal components to highly complex multi-component assemblies.

Our state-of-the-art 3,000 sq. m. campus combines advanced CAD/CAM design with progressive press stamping, multi-axis CNC machining, and automated flow-forming to meet the tightest OEM specifications.
