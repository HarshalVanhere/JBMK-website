Our Story

From Local Tooling Pioneer to Global OEM Supplier

JBMK Precision Components India Pvt. Ltd. is a leading manufacturer of precision components and sheet metal parts for the automotive, offroad, and industrial sectors.

Founded in 2012, JBMK Precision Components India Pvt. Ltd. (originally established as JBM Kanemitsu Pulley Pvt. Ltd.) was built with a singular vision: to deliver world-class precision automotive components, sheet metal components, and precision-engineered metal forming solutions for the automotive, offroad, and industrial sectors.

Over the past decade, we have continuously pushed the boundaries of precision metal forming, CNC machining, flow forming, and sheet metal manufacturing. As one of India’s leading sheet metal manufacturers and sheet metal pulley manufacturers, we pioneered the replacement of heavy raw cast pulleys with highly optimized, lightweight flow-formed sheet metal pulleys and precision transmission components. This innovation has helped leading automotive OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers reduce vehicle weight, improve fuel efficiency, enhance NVH (Noise, Vibration, and Harshness) performance, and optimize drivetrain efficiency.

Today, under the joint venture capabilities of JBM Group, JBMK stands as one of India’s most advanced precision component manufacturers, sheet metal manufacturers, and automotive OEM suppliers, operating state-of-the-art steel forming, CNC machining, precision stamping, roll forming, and automotive component manufacturing facilities. As a trusted global sheet metal pulley manufacturer and industrial components exporter, we supply high-quality precision pulleys, custom sheet metal parts, automotive transmission components, and engineered industrial metal components to Tier-1 suppliers and OEMs across the USA, Europe, Brazil, and more than 40 countries worldwide.
