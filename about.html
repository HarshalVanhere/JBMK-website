Our Story

From Local Tooling Pioneer to Global OEM Supplier

Founded in 2012, JBMK Precision Components India Pvt. Ltd. (originally established as JBM Kanemitsu Pulley Pvt. Ltd.) was built with a singular vision: to deliver world-class precision-formed components that power the automotive and industrial sectors.

Over the past three decades, we have continuously pushed the boundaries of metal-forming technology. By replacing heavy, raw cast pulleys with highly optimized, lightweight, flow-formed sheet metal parts, we have helped leading OEMs reduce vehicle weight, improve fuel efficiency, and lower NVH (Noise, Vibration, and Harshness) metrics.

Today, as a key manufacturer under the joint venture capabilities of JBM Group, we operate one of India's most advanced steel-forming and CNC machining centers, exporting precision pulleys to Tier-1 suppliers and OEMs in the USA, Europe, Brazil, and over 40 countries globally.

Milestones

Timeline of Innovation

A look at the key moments that shaped our journey over the years.

2012

Foundation

Inception of the company with a focus on manufacturing V-Belt pulleys for agricultural and industrial clients.

2004

OEM Breakthrough

Inaugurated dedicated automotive production lines and secured first major Tier-1 supply agreement with Maruti Suzuki.

2012

Plant Expansion

Relocated to our current 3,000 sq. m. campus, integrating advanced progressive stamping presses and a Metrology lab.

2016

IATF & Global Exports

Obtained IATF 16949 certification and initiated direct exports to major automotive OEMs in Europe and North America.

2021

Technology Shift

Pioneered advanced flow-forming and spin-forming technologies, facilitating complex sheet-metal pulley conversions.

2026

Today

Operating as JBMK Precision Components, supplying a comprehensive catalog of 1,200+ variants to 2,500+ global clients.
