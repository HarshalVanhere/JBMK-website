Damper Housing

Robust housings engineered for effective vibration damping and isolation. Damper housings encapsulate the elastomeric or viscous elements of torsional vibration dampers (TVD). Our housings are stamped and machined with high geometric accuracy to ensure seamless integration and balanced rotation.

Performance Engineering Highlights

  • High roundness and concentricity to support rubber/silicone filling
  • Thick-gauge steel to handle high torsional fatigue loads
  • Superior surface adhesion properties for rubber vulcanization
  • Dynamic unbalance checked at 100%

Standard Applications

  • Torsional Vibration Dampers (TVD)
  • Crankshaft Dampers
  • Driveline Vibration Isolators
