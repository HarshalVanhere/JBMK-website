What Makes a Pulley "Poly-V"

Multiple Ribs, One Continuous Belt

A Poly-V (also called multi-rib or micro-V) pulley carries several narrow, parallel V-shaped grooves across a single wide belt, instead of one deep groove per belt as on a conventional V-belt pulley. The larger combined contact area lets one continuous serpentine belt drive several accessories — alternator, power steering pump, A/C compressor, water pump — off a single crankshaft pulley, with less belt slip and a smaller overall drive package.

JBMK produces Poly-V grooves using precision fine-stamping and CNC flow-forming, holding pitch and groove-angle tolerances tight enough to keep belt wear and drive noise (NVH) within OEM limits across the full production run, not just on first-off samples.
