Timing / Sensor Pulley

Precision-machined teeth and sensor wheels for exact engine synchronization. Timing pulleys and target wheels are critical for valve-to-piston timing and crankshaft position sensing. JBMK manufactures these components with ultra-precise pitch tolerances and integrated sensor teeth.

Performance Engineering Highlights

  • Perfect tooth pitch accuracy for slip-free timing
  • Integrated or projection-welded sensor/target wheels
  • Zero backlash capability for high-accuracy timing systems
  • Induction hardened tooth profile option for wear resistance

Standard Applications

  • Engine Timing Drives
  • Camshaft/Crankshaft Position Sensing Systems
  • Precision Automation Drives
