Precision Brackets

Stamped and machined mounting brackets with exact dimensional tolerances. Our structural brackets support critical engine and auxiliary components such as power steering pumps, alternators, and A/C compressors, guaranteeing rigid positioning under heavy vibration.

  • Precision hole positioning for easy vehicle assembly line fitment
  • Welded bushings and threaded inserts integration
  • Rust protection optimized for under-hood environmental exposure

Standard Applications

  • Alternator & Compressor Brackets
