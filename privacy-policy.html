Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit a Request for Quotation (RFQ), contact form, or email enquiry to JBMK Precision Components India Pvt. Ltd. ("JBMK", "we", "us"), we collect the information you provide directly: name, company name, email address, phone number, product interest, and any drawings or technical documents you choose to attach.

How We Use Information

Information submitted through this website is used solely to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, evaluate technical feasibility, and maintain records of our commercial correspondence. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

Technical Drawings & Confidential Information

Drawings and specifications submitted for a quotation are treated as confidential business information and are used only for the purpose of preparing your quotation and, where an order is placed, fulfilling that order. A Non-Disclosure Agreement is available on request for sensitive programs.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry and order-related information for as long as necessary to support the business relationship and to meet our quality-system record-keeping obligations under IATF 16949:2016.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have submitted to us by contacting jbmk.sales@jbmgroup.com.
