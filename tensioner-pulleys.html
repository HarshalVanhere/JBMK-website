Tensioner vs. Idler — What's the Difference

Two Different Jobs, Two Different Parts

A tensioner pulley is spring-loaded or hydraulically actuated and continuously adjusts to keep belt tension within spec as the belt stretches with age and the engine shifts on its mounts. An idler pulley is fixed in position and exists only to route or wrap the belt around obstructions in the drive layout — it carries no tensioning mechanism.

JBMK manufactures both as distinct product lines built for their respective duty cycles. If your requirement is a fixed guide/routing pulley rather than an automatic tensioner, see our Sleeve / Idler Pulley page.
